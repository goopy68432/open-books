> Ajaan Fuang once called concentration practice “the sport of wise people.” Like any sport, it’s something you want to do well but you also want to _enjoy_ yourself while you’re doing it. So you’ve got to find that right balance between sticking with it, mastering the technique, and having some fun in the process. Otherwise it gets grim and serious. Of course, our purpose _is_ serious, the practice is serious—we’re dealing with a big problem, the problem of suffering—but if you’re _grim_ about the path, that grimness begins to grind you down. So you have to learn how to develop a light attitude around what you’re doing
