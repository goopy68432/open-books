> _T_ _atra tatrabhinandini—_ delighting now here, now there: That’s part of the description of the craving that gives rise to suffering. It has a location, a here or a there. It focuses on a spot, and in that spot it creates a little kernel of what you want. Around that kernel comes your sense of who you are who’s able to get that thing, and the world in which you’re going to get that thing. That’s how becoming comes from craving
