> There are people who say that the idea of an independent self is the root of all evil. As long as you have the idea that you’re somehow separate, that you’re not totally dependent on everyone else, you’re going to be selfish. You’ll try to amass as much as you can for yourself and push other people away unless they can serve your selfish interests. But that’s not always the case
