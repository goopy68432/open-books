> When the Buddha defines concentration, or _samadhi,_ he defines it as _cittass’ekaggata._ Sometimes that term is translated as one-pointedness of mind. _Eka_ would be one, and _agga_ is often thought to mean point, but _agga_ doesn’t mean point. It means the summit of something, like the ridge on a roof or the summit of a mountain. It also means a gathering place, which seems to be the translation closest to what the Buddha’s trying to get at here. There’s a gathering place for the uposatha, there’s a gathering place for meals—all those are _aggas._
