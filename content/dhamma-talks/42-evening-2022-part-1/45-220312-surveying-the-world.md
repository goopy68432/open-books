> Sometimes it seems like the Buddha had all the fun. In his meditation, he got to survey the world—the whole world. As for us, he tells us when we meditate: Focus on your breath. We don’t get to move around that much. We don’t get to expand our horizons. We have to _narrow_ our horizons
