> _Vitakka_ and _vicara_ are two Pali words that mean thinking. They’re classified as verbal fabrication. In other words, you engage in these two activities—thinking _of_ something first and then thinking _about_ it, or directing your thoughts to a topic and then commenting on it—and then you break into speech. Without having had those verbal thoughts in the mind, there would be no external verbal action. They’re the mental activities that precede speech
