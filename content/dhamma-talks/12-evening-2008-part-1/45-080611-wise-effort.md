> The Thai idiom for meditation is making an effort _: tham khwaam phian._ And the important thing, of course, is to make the effort right. Brute force is not going to take nibbana by storm. As Ajaan Fuang once said, if you could get to nibbana simply through effort, we all would have been there by now. You have to make the effort, _right_ effort. This involves an element of wisdom and discernment
