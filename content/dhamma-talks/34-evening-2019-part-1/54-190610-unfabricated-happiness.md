> When the Buddha taught breath meditation, he started out with four very simple steps. You discern long breathing, you discern short breathing, you try to breathe in and out aware of the entire body. Then, in the fourth step, he introduced a technical term: fabrication, or _sankhara._ You try to breathe in and out calming bodily fabrication. That’s another word for the in-and-out breath
