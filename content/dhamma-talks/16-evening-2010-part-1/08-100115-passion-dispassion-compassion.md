> There’s a Pali term, _raga,_ which is usually translated as passion. And there’s its opposite, _viraga,_ or dispassion. There are areas in which raga or passion is a good thing. Passion for the Dhamma is a good thing. The desire to practice, the desire to attain the results of the practice: having a passion for these things is something the Buddha actually encouraged
