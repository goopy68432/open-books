> A common meditation instruction is that you should sit with whatever comes up. But the important thing is that you have a good place to sit, a solid place to sit, so that whatever comes up doesn’t blow you away. Some things come up and they’re very strong. You need to be able to resist their strength, to have a way of counteracting them and, when you can, taking them apart
