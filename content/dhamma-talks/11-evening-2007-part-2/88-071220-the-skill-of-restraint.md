> People often ask how to bring the practice into daily life. The answer is relatively simple. It’s one many people don’t like to hear, but it _is_ simple: restraint. There are basically two kinds of restraint. There’s restraint in what you do, and restraint in how you look and listen and smell and taste and feel and think about things — in other words, restraint in what goes out, and restraint in what comes in. And both kinds of restraint require a good amount of skill
