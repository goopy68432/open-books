> There’s a term in the Pali, s _appurisa,_ that can be translated in a lot of different ways: a person of integrity, a mature person. Literally, it means a _true_ person. The Buddha often pairs it with a noble person. It’s the kind of person you want to associate with, the kind of person whose habits you want to pick up, the kind of person you want to become.
