> On the night of his awakening, the Buddha discovered that people’s lives, and particularly their experiences of pleasure and pain, were influenced by their acts of intention and acts of attention. The intentions, of course, were the intentions that drove their actions. These intentions, in turn, were influenced by what they paid attention to, and who they paid attention to. The _what_ was a matter of right views and wrong views. The _who_ concerned the issue of whether, in forming their views, they paid attention to the teachings of the noble ones
