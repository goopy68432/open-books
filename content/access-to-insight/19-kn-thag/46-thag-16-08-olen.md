He who once lived in negligence And then is negligent no more, He's the one who brightens this world — Like the moon released from a cloud. Who follows up with wholesome deeds Unwholesome deeds he may have done, He's the one who brightens this world — Like the moon released from a cloud. Indeed that youthful bhikkhu who Pours himself into the Buddha's teaching, He's the one who brightens this world — Like the moon released from a cloud.
