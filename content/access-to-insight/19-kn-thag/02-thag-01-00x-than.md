# Subhuti (Thag 1.1) {[Thag 1](<./../../../tipitaka/sltp/Th_utf8.html#v.1> "Read the Pali source")}

My hut is roofed, comfortable, free of drafts; my mind, well-centered, set free. I remain ardent. So, rain-deva. Go ahead & rain.
