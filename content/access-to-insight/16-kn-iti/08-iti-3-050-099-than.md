## § 50. {Iti 3.1; [Iti 44](<./../../../tipitaka/sltp/It_utf8.html#pts.044> "Read the Pali source")}

_This was said by the Blessed One, said by the Arahant, so I have heard:_ "There are these three roots of what is unskillful. Which three? Greed as a root of what is unskillful, aversion as a root of what is unskillful, delusion as a root of what is unskillful. These are the three roots of what is unskillful."

Greed, aversion, delusion destroy the self-same person of evil mind from whom they are born, like the fruiting of the bamboo.
