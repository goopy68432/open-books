# Thag 1:6 Dabba (“Capable”)

Whoever,

hard to tame,

has been tamed by taming,

—capable, contented,

crossed over doubt,

victorious, as his fears

are dispersed:

_He_ is capable,

unbound,

steadfast in mind.
