# Thag 1:16 Belaṭṭhasīsa

Just as a fine thoroughbred steed,

with swishing tail & mane

runs with next-to-no effort,

so my days & nights

run with next-to-no effort

now that I’ve gained a pleasure

not of the flesh.1

Note

1\. See [SN 36:31](</suttas/SN/SN36_31.html>) and [Thag 1:85](</suttas/KN/Thag/thag1_85.html>).
