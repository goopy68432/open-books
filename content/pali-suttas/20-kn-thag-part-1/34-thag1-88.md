# Thag 1:88 Ajjuna

Able was I

to raise myself from water

to dry land.1

Swept along by the great flood,

I penetrated

the truths.2

Notes

1\. A standard image for the practice is that of making one’s way across a river from its dangerous near shore to the safety of the further shore. For other uses of this image, see [SN 35:197](</suttas/SN/SN35_197.html>), [AN 4:5](</suttas/AN/AN4_5.html>), [AN 7:15](</suttas/AN/AN7_15.html>), and [Sn 5](</suttas/KN/StNp/StNp5_intro.html>).

2\. The four noble truths.
