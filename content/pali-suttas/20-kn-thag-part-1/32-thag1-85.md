# Thag 1:85 Sunāga

Adept in a theme for the mind,

sensing the savor of solitude,

practicing jhāna,

masterful, mindful,

you’d attain a pleasure

not of the flesh.1

Note

1\. See [SN 36:31](</suttas/SN/SN36_31.html>).
