# Thag 1:57 Kuṭivihārin (2)

This was your old hut,

and you aspire to another,

new hut.

Discard your hope for a hut, monk.

A new hut will be

painful all over again.1

Note

1\. See [Thag 2:32, note 1](</suttas/KN/Thag/thag2_32.html#thag2.32note01>).
