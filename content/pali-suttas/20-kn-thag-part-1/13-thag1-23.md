# Thag 1:23 Gosāla

I—having eaten honey-rice

in a bamboo patch

and rightly grasping the aggregates’1

arising-disbanding—

will return to the hillside, intent

on seclusion.

Note

1\. The aggregates of form, feeling, perception, fabrication, and consciousness. See [SN 22:48](</suttas/SN/SN22_48.html>), [SN 22:59](</suttas/SN/SN22_59.html>), and [SN 22:79](</suttas/SN/SN22_79.html>).
