# Thag 1:10 Puṇṇamāsa

Whoever,

a master of knowing,

contented,1

restrained in mind,

destroys longing for here & beyond,

unsmeared with regard to all dhammas,

would know the arising-&-disbanding

of the world.

Note

1\. Reading _santusito_ with the Thai edition. Other editions have _samito_ —calmed, appeased—which doesn’t fit the meter.
