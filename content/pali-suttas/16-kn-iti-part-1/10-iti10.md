# Itivuttaka 10—13

_This was said by the Blessed One, said by the Arahant, so I have heard:_ “Monks, one who has not fully known & fully understood aversion… delusion… anger… contempt, whose mind has not been cleansed of passion for it, has not abandoned it, is incapable of putting an end to stress. But one who has fully known & fully understood aversion… delusion… anger… contempt, whose mind has been cleansed of passion for it and has abandoned it, is capable of putting an end to stress.

_[The verses for these discourses are identical with those for[§§2—5](</suttas/KN/Iti/iti2.html>).]_
