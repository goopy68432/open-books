# Fettered  
Saññojana Sutta (SN 1:64)

“With what is the world fettered?

What is its exploration?

With the abandoning

of what

is there said,

‘unbinding’?”

The Buddha:

“Fettered with delight is the world.

Directed thought is its exploration.

With the abandoning

of craving

is there said,

‘unbinding.’”
