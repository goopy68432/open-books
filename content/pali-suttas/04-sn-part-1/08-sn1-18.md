# Shame  
Hiri Sutta (SN 1:18)

_This pair of verses provides an answer to the question posed and left unanswered in[Dhp 143](</suttas/KN/Dhp/Ch10.html#dhp143>)._

“Who in the world

is a man constrained by shame,

who awakens to censure

like a fine stallion to the whip?”

“Those restrained by shame

are rare—

those who go through life

always mindful.

Having reached the end

of suffering & stress,

they go among the discordant

harmoniously.”
