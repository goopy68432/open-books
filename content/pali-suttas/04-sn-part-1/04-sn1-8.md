# Thoroughly Confused  
Susammuṭṭha Sutta (SN 1:8)

_Like the similar sutta[SN 1:7](</suttas/SN/SN1_7.html>), this sutta offers evidence in support of the translation, “awakened” for the Pali term,_ Buddha. _The verses draw a direct connection between two words derived from the same root: the adjective_ sambuddha, _a variant of_ buddha, _and the verb_ pābujjhati, _which in context clearly means to awaken from sleep._

Standing to one side, a devatā addressed the Blessed One with a verse:
    
    
    “Those for whom
    phenomena are thoroughly confused,
    who may be led
    by the teachings of others:
    	Asleep are they;
    	they haven’t awakened.
    It’s time for them
    to awaken.”

The Buddha:
    
    
    “Those for whom
    phenomena are not confused,
    may not be led
    by the teachings of others.
    	Awakened
    	through right knowing,
    they go among the discordant
    harmoniously.”1

1\. See [note 1 under SN 1:7](</suttas/SN/SN1_7.html#sn1.7note01>).
